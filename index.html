FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What are marble shop drawings?

Marble shop drawings are detailed fabrication and installation drawings for stone works — plans, elevations, sections, joint layouts, edge profiles, cut-outs, thickness and finish — prepared so fabricators and site teams in Dubai and the UAE can cut and install accurately.

Which areas of the UAE do you serve?

We work with contractors, fabricators, consultants and designers in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah and across the UAE. Most work is delivered digitally, so location is not a barrier.

What file formats do you deliver?

AutoCAD DWG and PDF drawing sets as standard. 3D renderings are delivered as high-resolution images. Other formats can be discussed when you request a quote.

What do I need to send for a quote?

Architectural or interior drawings, sketches, site dimensions, photos or a short description of the scope and deadline. Send them through the enquiry form on our contact page or by email to dxb3ddeaign@gmail.com.

Do you provide drawings in Arabic?

Yes. We communicate in English and Arabic, and drawing notes can be prepared in English, Arabic or both.

How much does a contractor website cost?

Our Contractor Website package is AED 2,000 for a 5-page, mobile-friendly website with WhatsApp button and enquiry form. 50% is paid upfront and the balance on completion.
