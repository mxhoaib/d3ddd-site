Developers — Dubai & UAE

3D visualization for real estate project teams in the UAE

Presentation stills, material studies and design-coordination views for teams who need to see a space before they approve a drawing. The picture does not replace the shop drawings.

A development team often has to show a lobby, a typical apartment or a villa majlis before the shop drawings are finished. A still render can carry the vein direction, the joinery colour and the light well enough for that meeting. It cannot be measured with a tape, and it should not be the document a factory cuts from. D3DDD builds those images from the drawings you send, for project teams working in Dubai and across the UAE.

This page is about visualization, presentations and design coordination. It is not a marketing retainer, and it does not claim placements, sales results or a relationship with any developer. A single 3D still render is 650 AED, with 50% upfront. Additional views, models and walkthroughs are quoted per view or per space after the brief is clear.

What a presentation still is for

A presentation still is a decision picture. It is useful when a project team, a sales gallery or an internal design review needs to compare two stones, two joinery colours or two ceiling heights. The quotation names the number of views. A lobby, a living room and a close-up of a marble wall are three views, not an open-ended set of “enough images”. An extra view later is priced on its own. If the slabs arrive and the vein does not match the picture, the picture was a study. The supply contract is still the approved shop drawing and the stone that was actually delivered.

Files are PNG or JPG at the resolution written in the quotation. A model file is included only if you ask for it, for example so another office can place a camera later. A short walkthrough is a separate line, not an assumption that every still comes with a film. Notes can point back to the drawing sheet the camera was taken from, so the picture does not drift away from the plan.

Design coordination, not a substitute for dimensions

Design coordination views are the images that show a junction: stone meeting joinery, a ceiling bulkhead against a curtain wall, a vanity against a fluted wall. They are useful because a plan hides that corner. They are dangerous if the corner in the picture uses a thickness the drawings do not. When visualization and shop drawings are in the same quotation, the image follows the joint logic of the sheets. When only the image is commissioned, it is labelled as a study based on the drawings you sent, and those drawings may still change.

In Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah a client meeting may approve the look weeks before the consultant approves the shop drawings for fabrication. If a splash height or a niche moves in the approved drawing and the picture is not updated, the site is showing an image that is no longer true. An update of the still is described in the quotation rather than assumed. D3DDD does not treat a render as an authority submission or as a permit.

What the model is built from

The model needs a plan, even a sketch with approximate sizes, before it is worth building. Photographs of the space help. If the stone has already been delivered, photographs of the slabs help the vein. Materials you are still choosing can be shown as options: two stones, or a stone and a timber, in the same camera. Inventing a floor plate, a view or a neighbouring building that you did not send is not part of the work. If a drawing is missing, that part of the image waits or is shown as a simple mass, and the quotation says which.

How this sits with the rest of the studio

Architectural design is the earlier plan work: layouts, space planning and elevations in DWG, so a later shop drawing does not have to be traced from a picture. Marble shop drawings are the fabrication set for floors, vanities, stairs and cladding. Fit-out drawings cover the joinery the image is showing. A developer’s technical office can ask for the stills first and the sheets second, or the other way around. They are different deliverables. The 3D rendering in Dubai service lists the file types in more detail.

The portfolio is shop drawings rather than a gallery of marketing renders. The entrance-lobby medallion and the villa ground-floor layout are useful references because they are the kind of stone geometry a presentation still has to respect: a radial hall, a waterjet centre, a numbered slab set-out. Look at them before you brief a view of a similar floor. They are issued sheets with client marks removed, not a claim that the studio produced a sales campaign.

What to send for a quotation

Send the plans or sketches, the materials you are considering, the rooms or elevations you need to see, and whether the images are for an internal review or for a presentation you will show onward. Name the emirate if the review context matters, even though the files themselves are delivered digitally. A deadline belongs in the same note. The date is confirmed with the price, before any modelling starts. Comment rounds on the images are further issues once your marked-up PDF or your written notes arrive.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Will the renders be enough to fabricate from?

No. Fabrication needs DWG and PDF shop drawings with dimensions, joints and cut-outs. The still is for the decision about the look.

Can you show more than one material?

Yes. The quotation states how many options and how many cameras. Two stones in one view is a different scope from two fully different rooms.

Do you produce the sales brochure as well?

No. The deliverable is the stills, and a model or a walkthrough only if the quotation says so. Layout of a brochure is not part of this service.

Which drawings should we send first?

The plans and the elevations you want the camera to follow. If those are still sketches, the image is labelled as a study.
