# Villa Swimming Pool & Outdoor Seating Tiling Details – Dubai

*By Shoaib Hussan, Founder of D3DDD*

Tiling and stone detailing for the external works of a private villa in Dubai, prepared by Shoaib Hussan, Founder of D3DDD: a swimming pool, a raised pool deck with a sunken seating area and fireplace, a water pond, and the entrance veranda.

## Scope

This project is tagged as [architectural drafting](https://d3ddd.com/services/architectural-drafting/), not as a marble shop-drawing pack. The set is for the external works of a private villa in Dubai. It includes:

- The swimming pool
- A raised pool deck with a sunken seating area and fireplace
- A water pond
- The entrance veranda

The work starts with a fully dimensioned external plan. Every 1200×600 mm floor tile and every 1200×380 mm pool coping piece is set out from fixed grid lines. Finished floor levels and pool depths are marked throughout. The coping, steps and edges on this set are the kind of stone information collected in the [Marble Shop Drawings Guide (UAE)](/blog/what-are-marble-shop-drawings/).

## Challenge

External pool and deck tiling has to close on a grid, match coping pieces around a pool edge, and stay true at steps, a sunken seat and a fireplace. If the set-out is loose, the installer cuts many tiles on site and the joint lines drift. This set is drawn so the tiling contractor can install with very little cutting on site — that outcome is stated on the page.

Levels matter as much as the plan: finished floor levels and pool depths are marked throughout, and long sections cut through the deep and shallow ends and the seating area.

## Drawings delivered

Deliverables:

- An external flooring plan
- A sitting-area plan
- Three long sections
- Four construction details

Long sections through the pool, the deep and shallow ends and the seating area show how the cladding, coping and steps work together. Enlarged details at 1:10 and 1:20 cover the pool steps, bull-nose and pencil-polish edges, glue-fixed tiles on concrete bedding, the fireplace cladding and the built-in seating.

The gallery shows 4 sheets. The deliverable list (two plans, three long sections, four details) is larger than four images, so the photographs are likely a selection.

## Software

AutoCAD 2021, as listed on the page.

## Outcome

The drawings were coordinated with the design intent so the tiling contractor could install with very little cutting on site. That is the published result. No handover date, client name or finished-site photograph is on the page.
