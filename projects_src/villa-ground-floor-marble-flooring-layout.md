# Luxury Villa Ground Floor Marble & Stone Flooring Layout – Dubai

*By Shoaib Hussan, Founder of D3DDD*

A colour-zoned flooring layout for the full ground floor of a large luxury villa in Dubai, prepared by Shoaib Hussan, Founder of D3DDD, as a design option for client approval.

## Scope

Unlike most pages in this portfolio, this project is tagged as [architectural designing](https://d3ddd.com/services/architectural-designing/), not as marble shop drawings. It is a presentation-ready ground-floor flooring layout (Option 02), with material zoning and slab and tile set-out. It forms the base for the detailed marble shop drawings that follow.

The plan covers the full ground floor of a large luxury villa in Dubai. Each zone is drawn with its own finish:

- A radial marble grid in the circular entrance and living hall
- Large-format stone in the formal dining area and around the majlis
- Herringbone timber in the majlis and guest bedroom
- Coordinated tile layouts in the kitchen, pantry, storage and wet areas

Furniture is shown faintly so the pattern can be judged against the real room layout. Borders, thresholds and changes of material are drawn at every doorway.

The stone types in each zone are described as radial marble, large-format stone, herringbone timber and tile.

## Challenge

The entrance and living hall are circular, with a curved staircase and a faceted outer wall. A radial marble grid has to follow those curves, and the joint lines have to stay symmetrical about the main axes. If the setting-out is off, cuts will look uneven once the floor is laid.

The rest of the ground floor changes material several times — stone, timber and tile — so every doorway needs a drawn border or threshold. The layout is a design option (Option 02) for approval, so it also has to be readable as a presentation sheet, not only as a technical plan.

## Drawings delivered

Deliverables: one presentation-ready ground floor flooring layout (Option 02) with material zoning and slab and tile set-out.

The radial layout follows the curved staircase and the faceted outer wall. Joint lines are set out so cuts stay symmetrical about the main axes. The gallery shows 3 sheets.

The published outcome is a colour-coded, presentation-ready Option 02 ground-floor layout that the client can approve before detailed marble shop drawings are prepared.
