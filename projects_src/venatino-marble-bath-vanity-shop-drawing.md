# Venatino Marble Freestanding Bath Vanity Shop Drawing – Dubai

*By Shoaib Hussan, Founder of D3DDD*

A fabrication shop drawing for a freestanding bathroom vanity in 2 cm Venatino marble, made for a private villa in Dubai, prepared by Shoaib Hussan, Founder of D3DDD.

## Scope

The vanity is 2200 mm long and stands on brass legs. It has an undermounted oval basin, a bottle shelf, a moulded fascia and a lower display shelf, all in 2 cm Venatino. The set is a fabrication shop drawing: every dimension is given for cutting and assembly.

The sheet includes:

- A front elevation
- A side section
- The vanity top plan
- The shelf top plan
- Four enlarged details, labelled D1 to D4
- A cutting plan with marble pieces placed on photos of the approved slabs

This is a typical [marble vanity shop drawing](https://d3ddd.com/marble.html): edges, the sink junction, supports and slab mapping are shown, not only an overall size. See also the [marble shop drawings](https://d3ddd.com/marble.html) service page.

## Challenge

A freestanding vanity has to stand, drain and look consistent in vein across several cut pieces. The junctions drawn in detail are moulding profiles, pencil-polish and full-round edges, the undermount sink junction, the backsplash and the support brackets (details D1 to D4).

The marble pieces are identified as C-01 to C-06 and S-01 to S-04 and are placed directly on photos of the approved slabs. That is how the client and fabricator confirm vein direction and matching before any cut is made.

## Drawings delivered

One fully dimensioned shop drawing sheet with elevations, sections, edge details and a slab-mapped cutting plan.

The gallery shows 3 sheets.

## Software

AutoCAD 2021, as listed on the page.

## Outcome

The published outcome is a fully dimensioned fabrication drawing for a 2200 mm freestanding Venatino vanity on brass legs, with piece marks C-01 to C-06 and S-01 to S-04 mapped onto approved slab photos so vein direction and matching can be confirmed before cutting. No fabrication or installation result is given on the page.
