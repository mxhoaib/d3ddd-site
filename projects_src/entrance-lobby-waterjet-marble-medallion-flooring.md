# Villa Entrance Lobby Waterjet Marble Medallion & Flooring Layout – Dubai

*By Shoaib Hussan, Founder of D3DDD*

Marble flooring shop drawings for the ground floor of a luxury villa in Dubai, prepared by Shoaib Hussan, Founder of D3DDD. The centrepiece is a waterjet-cut medallion in the entrance lobby, set inside a numbered ground-floor slab layout.

## Scope

The set covers [marble flooring shop drawings](https://d3ddd.com/blog/marble-flooring-wall-cladding-shop-drawings/) for the ground floor of a luxury villa in Dubai. The floor plan sets out Afyoun Sugar marble across the formal and informal living rooms, the dining room and the office. The entrance lobby holds a circular waterjet rosette, which is the visual focus of the floor.

Every slab on the floor plan is tagged with a piece number in the GF-xx series and placed using real slab images, so the vein flow can be checked before cutting. What a full marble shop-drawing set contains, beyond this lobby sheet, is the [Marble Shop Drawings Guide (UAE)](/blog/what-are-marble-shop-drawings/). That is the same method used on other D3DDD [marble shop drawings](https://d3ddd.com/marble.html) in this portfolio: the layout is not only dimensioned, it is tied to the actual stone.

Client details have been removed, as on the rest of the portfolio.

## Challenge

A waterjet medallion has to sit cleanly in a surrounding floor, with joints, borders and a threshold that can actually be cut and installed. The lobby sheet therefore has to do more than show a decorative rosette. It has to number every waterjet piece, describe the border and frame, and show how the medallion meets the main door and the staircase.

The surrounding rooms use Afyoun Sugar on a numbered slab layout. Vein direction across formal and informal living, dining and office areas has to be readable from the drawings before the yard starts cutting. Using real slab images on the plan is how that check is made on this set.

## Drawings delivered

The page lists these deliverables:

- A ground-floor marble flooring plan
- An entrance lobby medallion layout
- Piece schedule references
- A skirting detail

The entrance lobby sheet details a circular rosette with a rope border in Dark and Light Emperador. The rosette sits inside a Black Titanium frame with 10 mm brass inlay strips. Every waterjet piece is numbered WJ-xx for fabrication. The same sheet covers hair-joint layout, the main door threshold, the transition into the staircase, and a flush skirting section.

The drawings are ready for CNC and waterjet cutting and for installation on site. Three sheets are shown in the gallery.

## Software

AutoCAD 2021, as listed on the page.

## Outcome

The published outcome is a coordinated floor and medallion set: Afyoun Sugar slabs numbered GF-xx on real slab images, a waterjet rosette numbered WJ-xx with Dark and Light Emperador rope border, a Black Titanium frame and 10 mm brass inlay, plus threshold, staircase transition and flush skirting detail, issued so fabrication and installation can proceed from the drawings. No on-site installation result, date or client comment is given on the page, and none is added here.
