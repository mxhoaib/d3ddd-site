# Marble Slab Cutting Layout & Bathroom Cladding Drawings – Dubai

*By Shoaib Hussan, Founder of D3DDD*

A slab-optimised cutting layout for a first-floor bathroom clad in Venatino marble, for a private villa in Dubai, prepared by Shoaib Hussan, Founder of D3DDD.

## Scope

The work starts with dimensioned wall panels numbered 01 to 10. It includes casing, plinth block and dado profiles, and a brick-bond tile layout of 300 mm modules with numbered pieces.

Every piece is then nested onto photos of the real slabs from the supplier’s bundle, one sheet per slab. The fabricator gets an exact cutting map, and the client can approve vein placement before the stone is cut. Moulding, chamfer-edge and skirting strips are taken from the offcuts.

This is the project-level version of the method described in the [Marble Shop Drawings Guide (UAE)](/blog/what-are-marble-shop-drawings/), in D3DDD’s guides to [slab allocation and cutting optimization](https://d3ddd.com/blog/marble-slab-allocation-cutting-optimization/) and [slab nesting and cutting lists](https://d3ddd.com/blog/slab-nesting-cutting-lists-gulf/), and in the [marble shop drawings](https://d3ddd.com/marble.html) service.

The stone is Venatino, on a first-floor bathroom of a private villa in Dubai.

## Challenge

Bathroom panels have to read as one vein-matched room, while still using the real slabs in the bundle efficiently. The layout keeps waste low and holds the vein direction across adjoining walls. Offcuts are not treated as scrap: moulding, chamfer-edge and skirting strips are taken from them.

Nesting on photographs of the actual supplier slabs — one sheet per slab — is how both the fabricator and the client see the cutting map before anyone cuts.

## Drawings delivered

The drawings include:

- Wall panel elevations with sizes
- Profile details
- A numbered piece layout for each wall
- Slab-by-slab cutting plans on real slab images, ready for CNC cutting and installation

Panels are numbered 01 to 10, with casing, plinth block and dado profiles and a 300 mm brick-bond module layout. The gallery shows 3 sheets.

## Software

AutoCAD 2021, as listed on the page.

## Outcome

The published outcome is a numbered, slab-photographed cutting plan for Venatino bathroom panels, described as low-waste and vein-matched, with offcuts used for moulding, chamfer-edge and skirting, ready for CNC cutting and installation. No measured waste percentage or installation photograph is published, and none is invented here.
