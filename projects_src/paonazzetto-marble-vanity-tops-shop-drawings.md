# Paonazzetto Marble Vanity Tops Shop Drawings – Residential Villa, Dubai

*By Shoaib Hussan, Founder of D3DDD*

Shop drawings for seven undermount bathroom vanity tops in 2 cm Paonazzetto marble, for a residential villa in Dubai, prepared by Shoaib Hussan, Founder of D3DDD.

## Scope

The set covers seven vanity tops, each with a top view and a front view showing the countertop, a 100 mm skirting and a 320 mm fascia. The drawings mark overall sizes, sink centres, R120 radius corners and the wall conditions.

A shared detail sheet explains how the vanities are built. It covers:

- The undermounted sink section
- Pencil-round and 2 mm edge profiles
- A 5×10 mm drip groove
- Angle support brackets
- The fascia-to-top junction with no overlap

The marble faces are drawn with an image of the actual Paonazzetto slab, so the veining can be seen at true scale and the fabricator can plan cuts to keep the vein flow consistent from one bathroom to the next.

This is a multi-bathroom vanity package of the kind described in the [Marble Shop Drawings Guide (UAE)](/blog/what-are-marble-shop-drawings/) and prepared under [marble shop drawings](https://d3ddd.com/marble.html), alongside single-unit fabrication drawings such as the [Venatino freestanding vanity](https://d3ddd.com/projects/venatino-marble-bath-vanity-shop-drawing/).

## Challenge

Seven tops in the same stone have to look consistent from room to room, while each top still fits its own wall conditions, sink centres and corners. The shared detail sheet keeps construction identical (undermount sink, edges, drip groove, brackets, fascia junction). The true-scale slab image is how vein flow is planned across the bathrooms before cutting.

R120 radius corners, a 100 mm skirting and a 320 mm fascia have to be drawn the same way on every unit so the workshop does not interpret each top differently.

## Drawings delivered

Two A3 shop drawing sheets covering all vanity tops, sink details and edge profiles, issued for approval and fabrication.

The gallery shows 3 sheets.

## Software

AutoCAD 2021, as listed on the page.

## Outcome

The published outcome is two A3 shop drawing sheets for seven 2 cm Paonazzetto undermount vanity tops, with sink and edge details and true-scale slab previews, issued for approval and fabrication so vein flow can stay consistent from one bathroom to the next. No installation result is on the page.
