# Fusion Blue Marble Door Frames & Skirting Slab Layout – Dubai

*By Shoaib Hussan, Founder of D3DDD*

Shop drawings and a slab cutting plan for Fusion Blue marble door frames, wall trims and profiled skirting in a private villa in Dubai, prepared by Shoaib Hussan, Founder of D3DDD.

## Scope

The set covers Fusion Blue door frames, lintels, wall trims and profiled skirting. Fusion Blue has a strong, flowing vein, so piece placement on the slab matters as much as the dimensions. Piece marks and slab layouts of this kind are part of the [Marble Shop Drawings Guide (UAE)](/blog/what-are-marble-shop-drawings/).

The elevations set out every door frame (D-01, D-02 and onward), lintel, wall return and skirting run (SK-01 onward) with exact sizes. Frame return, adhesive fixing, pencil-polish edge and skirting profile details are included.

Each numbered piece is then nested onto images of the three approved slabs, with door-frame jambs, lintels and skirting strips arranged to follow the vein direction.

This is another slab-nested [marble shop drawings](https://d3ddd.com/marble.html) package, close in method to the [Venatino bathroom cutting layout](https://d3ddd.com/projects/marble-slab-cutting-layout-bathroom-cladding/) but applied to frames and skirting rather than wall panels. See also [slab allocation and cutting optimization](https://d3ddd.com/blog/marble-slab-allocation-cutting-optimization/).

## Challenge

A strongly veined stone will look broken if jambs, lintels and skirting are cut without a slab plan. Placement on the slab matters as much as the dimensions. The nesting on three approved slab images is how every piece is arranged to follow the vein, and how the client can approve the look visually before production.

The elevations still have to carry exact sizes for D-01, D-02 and onward, lintels, wall returns and SK-01 onward, plus the frame return, adhesive fixing, pencil-polish edge and skirting profile. The cutting plan does not replace those details.

## Drawings delivered

The drawings include:

- Elevation drawings with a piece schedule
- Edge and profile details
- Three slab-by-slab cutting layouts on real slab images
- Matching line-drawing versions for the workshop

The result is a cutting plan the fabricator can follow directly and the client can approve visually before production, with door frames, lintels and skirting nested slab by slab so every piece follows the vein. No installation photograph or later comment is published.
