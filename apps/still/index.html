Still — Free Online Image Straightener & Photo Leveler

Still is a free, privacy-first web application that straightens crooked photos, fixes tilted horizons, crops images to any aspect ratio, adds styled text captions, and batch-exports everything as a ZIP archive — 100% on-device, with photos that never leave your computer or phone.

Key features

  • Auto horizon detection — automatically finds level lines and corrects tilt with one click
  • Fine rotation — adjust the angle from −15° to +15° in 0.1° increments, plus 90° rotation buttons
  • Auto-crop & ratio presets — 3:2, 4:3, 16:9, 1:1, 4:5, 9:16 or any custom pixel size, with a rule-of-thirds grid
  • Perspective & keystone correction with horizontal and vertical flips
  • Tilt-shift focus band with adjustable feathering and blur
  • Auto-enhance brightness and contrast based on image histogram analysis, plus 7 color looks
  • Multi-layer text captions with shadow, outline, and label effects, drag-and-resize placement
  • Batch queue — process dozens of photos, auto-align all, and download a ZIP
  • Full undo/redo history (60 states) with keyboard shortcuts
  • Zero uploads — everything runs locally in your browser

How to straighten a tilted photo

  1. Open this page and drop your photo into the preview area (or click “Add images”).
  2. Click “Detect horizon for this image” for automatic straightening, or use the rotation slider.
  3. Optionally crop, auto-enhance, or add a caption.
  4. Click “Download selected” — your levelled image is saved instantly.

Frequently asked questions

Is Still free?

Yes, completely free with no registration or limits.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Processing uses HTML5 Canvas inside your browser; files never leave your device.

Which formats are supported?

PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF, BMP, and AVIF, exporting as JPEG or PNG.

Can I straighten many photos at once?

Yes. Queue multiple photos, click “Auto-align all”, and export everything as a ZIP.
