Paonazzetto Marble Vanity Tops Shop Drawings – Residential Villa, Dubai

Seven undermount Paonazzetto vanity tops with sink sections, edge profiles and true-scale slab previews.

By Shoaib Hussan, Founder of D3DDD

Shop drawings for seven undermount bathroom vanity tops in 2 cm Paonazzetto marble, for a residential villa in Dubai. Each vanity has a top view and a front view showing the countertop, the 100 mm skirting and the 320 mm fascia. The drawings also mark the overall sizes, sink centres, R120 radius corners and the wall conditions.

A shared detail sheet explains how the vanities are built. It covers the undermounted sink section, the pencil-round and 2 mm edge profiles, the 5×10 mm drip groove, the angle support brackets and the fascia-to-top junction with no overlap.

The marble faces are drawn with an image of the actual Paonazzetto slab. The client can see the veining at true scale, and the fabricator can plan the cuts to keep the vein flow consistent from one bathroom to the next.

Deliverables: two A3 shop drawing sheets covering all vanity tops, sink details and edge profiles, issued for approval and fabrication.
