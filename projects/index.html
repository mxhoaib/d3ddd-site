Portfolio — Dubai & UAE

Marble shop drawings and stone projects in Dubai

This portfolio shows selected marble and stone shop drawings, slab layouts and tiling details prepared by Shoaib Hussan / D3DDD for villas and residences in Dubai. The set includes waterjet medallion and flooring layouts, bathroom cladding, vanity fabrication drawings, slab-by-slab cutting plans, door-frame and skirting nesting, and external pool and terrace tiling.

Each project page explains the scope, the drawings delivered and the software used. Client details have been removed from every sheet.

These sheets are typical of the marble and stone shop drawings D3DDD prepares for contractors, fabricators and designers in Dubai and the UAE: plans, elevations, sections, edge profiles, piece numbers and cutting layouts that can be reviewed before stone is cut. Two of the projects are tagged on their own pages as architectural designing (the ground-floor flooring layout) and architectural drafting (the pool and outdoor tiling set), rather than as marble shop drawings.

If you have a villa or interior stone package that needs a similar set, request a quote.
