**Marble shop drawings are made to get the design approved. Marble fabrication drawings are made so the workshop can cut and finish each piece.** On many projects the two are issued as one package, but they answer different questions, and mixing them up leads to rework.

## Quick comparison

| | Shop drawings | Fabrication drawings |
|---|---|---|
| Main purpose | Review and approval by the consultant or client | Cutting and finishing in the workshop |
| Main reader | Consultant, contractor, client | Stone fabricator, CNC or saw operator |
| Typical content | Layouts, patterns, joints, sections, fixing and installation details | Piece numbers, exact sizes, thickness, edges, finishes, cut-outs, cutting list |
| Level of detail | How the stone fits the building | How each piece is made |

## What do marble shop drawings contain?

- Floor, wall or cladding layouts showing how the stone sits in the space.
- Joint lines, patterns and setting-out points.
- Sections and details at junctions, thresholds, corners and fixings.
- Material and finish references to be matched with approved samples.

They are submitted for review. The consultant may comment, and the drawings are revised until approved.

## What do marble fabrication drawings contain?

- A numbered drawing or entry for every piece.
- Final dimensions and thickness.
- Edge profiles and finishes for each side.
- Cut-outs such as sink openings, and any drilling or fixing preparation.
- A cutting list the workshop can work through.

## When does a project need both?

Use shop drawings whenever the consultant or contractor must approve how stone is arranged and installed. Add fabrication drawings when a workshop will cut the stone. For a small job, such as a single vanity top, one combined set can cover both. For floors, cladding and staircases, separate sets keep approval comments away from workshop instructions.

## Common mistakes

1. Sending approval drawings to the workshop before they are approved.
2. Cutting from a layout that has no piece numbers.
3. Forgetting edge and cut-out details until the stone is already cut.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are shop drawings and fabrication drawings the same thing?
No. Shop drawings are for approval; fabrication drawings are for cutting. They are often issued together.

### Who prepares them?
A drafter or the fabricator's technical team, usually in AutoCAD, shared as DWG and PDF.

### Can they be prepared remotely?
Yes, when design drawings, specifications and site measurements are available digitally.
