Images that help a decision, not a substitute for dimensions

3D rendering, modeling and visualization at D3DDD are the pictures and models clients and consultants look at before they approve a material or a layout. A still render can show vein direction, joinery colour and light. It cannot be measured with a tape. Fabrication still needs the DWG and PDF shop drawings. The two are often quoted together so the picture and the sheets describe the same design.

Typical work is an interior room, a marble feature, a façade study or a short walkthrough. Models are built from the drawings you send. If those drawings are still a sketch, the image is labelled as a study. The service is for designers, contractors and owners in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and the rest of the UAE, and it is priced per view or per space after the brief is clear.

What is delivered

  • PNG or JPG stills at the resolution agreed in the quote.
  • More than one material option when you are choosing stone or joinery finishes.
  • A model file on request, if a later drafter or renderer needs it.
  • A short camera path when a walkthrough is part of the scope.
  • Notes that point back to the drawing sheets, so the picture does not drift away from the dimensions.

DWG and PDF are not the deliverable of a render-only job. They are the deliverable of the drafting and marble services, which can be commissioned with the images. There is no published price per image on this site.

Where the images sit in a review

In Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah a render is often what the client approves, while the shop drawings are what the consultant approves for fabrication. Mixing those two decisions causes trouble: a vein that looked acceptable in a picture may be impossible on the slabs that arrived, and a picture with no joint lines does not tell the factory where to cut. When both are in the quote, the render is updated if the approved shop drawings change the design, and that update is described in the quote rather than assumed. The studio does not claim that a render is an authority submission. It is a decision tool.

Sheets that pair well with a material study include entrance lobby waterjet medallion, bathroom marble cladding and villa ground-floor flooring layout. FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do renders replace shop drawings?

No. A render shows the intent. Fabrication needs DWG and PDF shop drawings with dimensions, joints and cut-outs.

What files do I get?

PNG or JPG stills, a model file if you asked for one, and a walkthrough if that was in the quote. There is no standard price per image.

Can you match the marble drawings?

Yes, when both are commissioned. The image should follow the same material and the same joint logic as the sheets.

What do you need from me?

Drawings or a clear sketch, the materials you are considering, and the views you need. Photographs of the space help.

Is a render an approval document?

It helps a client decide. Consultant approval for fabrication is still done on the shop drawings. The studio does not treat a picture as a permit.
