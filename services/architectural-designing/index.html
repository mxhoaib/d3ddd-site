Design that can become a drawing

Architectural designing here is the step before fabrication drawings: concept plans, space planning, elevations and design development for villas, apartments, offices and fit-outs. The aim is a layout a client can approve and a drafter can later dimension. It is not a substitute for a signed consultancy appointment, and it is not a permit design unless that scope is written into the quote.

You might arrive with a brief and a plot or an apartment outline, or with a designer’s sketch that needs to be tested against furniture, circulation and wet areas. The work is done for owners, interior designers and contractors in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and the wider UAE. Fees are per project. Send the brief and any survey through the enquiry form.

What you receive

  • Plan options that test rooms, furniture and the feature zones you care about.
  • A developed plan once one option is chosen, with key dimensions.
  • Elevations and sections for finishes and heights, enough to brief stone and joinery.
  • A short note of materials where the design depends on them.
  • DWG and PDF, so the same file can move into shop drawings without being redrawn from a picture.

If the next step is fabrication, the developed drawings hand across to architectural drafting or to marble and stone shop drawings. If the client needs to see the material before they choose, 3D rendering can be quoted with the design. A flooring layout that began as a plan is in the portfolio as villa ground-floor flooring layout.

How this meets consultant review

Design drawings and shop drawings are often reviewed by different people, sometimes weeks apart. In Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah the early layout may be approved by the client and the interior designer, while the later shop drawings go to the project consultant for fabrication. Keeping the design in DWG means the shop-drawing set starts from the approved plan rather than from a traced PDF. D3DDD does not claim that a concept layout satisfies a municipality. If a project needs a permit package, that checklist is yours to send, and it is scoped separately from space planning.

Turnaround follows the same rule as the rest of the studio: the date is confirmed in writing with the price. Two plan options for an apartment are not the same duration as a villa with external majlis and pool. Comment rounds are further issues.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What do I receive from design work?

Plan options, a developed layout, and the elevations or sections needed to brief later drawings, in DWG and PDF. Renders are a separate service.

Is this a permit design?

Only if a permit package is written into the quote, and only from the requirements you send. A concept layout is not presented as an authority approval.

Can the design continue into shop drawings?

Yes. The DWG is the starting point for architectural drafting or marble shop drawings, quoted as the next step.

How long does a design take?

The date is confirmed with the quote. It depends on how many options you want and how complete the survey or brief is.

Do you work in Arabic?

Notes and communication can be in Arabic, English or both.

When should I use designing instead of drafting?

Use this page when the layout is still being decided: options, space planning and design development. Use architectural drafting when an approved design needs measured construction or shop drawings in AutoCAD.

Are 3D renders included in a design package?

No, unless the quote says so. 3D modeling and rendering is a separate service. A single still render has an owner-approved pack price of 650 AED (50% upfront); other views are quoted.

Can you start from a sketch or a survey only?

Yes. The live page already accepts a brief and a plot, an apartment outline, or a designer’s sketch. Completeness of that brief affects the delivery date, which is confirmed with the quote.

Do you do space planning for villas and apartments in Dubai?

Yes. The live service lists villas, apartments, offices and F&B fit-outs, for clients in Dubai and across the UAE.
