How the five services fit together

D3DDD is one studio, so a project can move from a layout to measured drawings to quantities to a picture of the result without changing hands at every step. You do not have to buy all of it. Most enquiries are one service: a stone package, a fit-out set, a bill, a set of stills, or a website. The enquiry form is the right place to send whichever of those you need, with the drawings or the brief attached in the email that the form opens.

marble and stone shop drawings is the fabrication set for marble and other stone: plans, edges, cut-outs, cutting lists and slab layouts, in DWG and PDF. architectural drafting is the broader AutoCAD work: shop drawings, joinery, fit-out sheets and as-built records. architectural designing is the earlier plan work, space planning and elevations, still issued as DWG so it can become shop drawings later. architectural estimation measures those drawings into a bill or a material schedule. 3D rendering produces the stills and walkthroughs people approve before the factory cuts. website development builds the contractor’s own site, and it is the only line with published prices.

Shared deliverables

Drawing services are issued as AutoCAD DWG and PDF unless the quote says otherwise. The DWG is editable and layered. The PDF is what a consultant marks up and what a site prints. Spreadsheets are added when the scope includes a cutting list, a bill of quantities or a cost summary. Renders are PNG or JPG. Websites are pages, not drawings. Notes on drawings can be English, Arabic or both.

A typical path in Dubai, Abu Dhabi or Sharjah

A fit-out in any of these emirates usually has two different approvals, and they should not be confused. The client and the interior designer approve the look: the layout, the material, sometimes a render. The project consultant approves the shop drawings for fabrication: dimensions, joints, build-ups and the interfaces between trades. Comments come back on the PDF. The next issue raises the revision. What differs by emirate and by project is the title block, the transmittal, and whether more than one consultant reviews the set. D3DDD follows the checklist you send. The studio does not publish a municipal procedure as if it were the same in every city, and a shop drawing is not a building permit. Where a project also needs a permit package or a BIM submission, that scope is quoted separately. The blog notes on drawing-package notes for Dubai and Dubai BIM submission notes describe those topics without turning them into a promise.

Turnaround is agreed in the quote, in writing, before drafting starts. It depends on how many rooms or sheets are involved and on how complete the source files are. A comment round is timed when the comments arrive. Missing dimensions pause only the sheets they affect.

Prices, stated only where they exist

Shop drawings, drafting, design, estimation and 3D rendering are quoted per project. There is no day rate on this site. FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which service should I ask for?

Stone fabrication drawings: marble shop drawings. General fit-out or joinery CAD: architectural drafting. Layouts before detailing: architectural designing. A bill or take-off: architectural estimation. Pictures: 3D rendering. A company site: website development. If the job spans two of these, say so on the enquiry form.

What file will I receive?

Drawings: DWG and PDF. Quantities: a spreadsheet and a PDF. Renders: PNG or JPG. Websites: the live pages agreed in the package. Other formats only if they are written into the quote.

How does approval work?

You or your consultant comment on the PDF. The revision is reissued. For shop drawings in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah that reviewer is usually the project consultant named in the contract. Send their checklist if you have one. The studio does not issue permits.

How fast is it?

The date is confirmed with the quote. It is not a fixed public lead time. Comment rounds and missing site sizes move the later issues.

What is priced already?

Only websites: AED 1,200, AED 2,000 and AED 3,500+, with 50% upfront on those packages. All drawing and 3D work is quoted per project.

Do you work outside Dubai?

Yes. Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Umm Al Quwain, Fujairah and the rest of the UAE. Delivery is digital.
