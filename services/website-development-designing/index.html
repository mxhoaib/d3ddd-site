Websites, with the prices that are actually published

Website development at D3DDD is for contractors, workshops and studios that need a clear site and a way for visitors to enquire. It is separate from shop drawings. Drawing work is quoted per project. Website work uses the packages already listed on this site.

  • Starter: CAD 599 or AED 1,200. One page, mobile layout, contact form, basic SEO.
  • Business: CAD 999 or AED 2,000. Up to five pages, with the same mobile layout, form and basic SEO. This is the contractor website package, 50% upfront and the balance on completion.
  • Premium: CAD 1,799+ or AED 3,500+. More pages and custom features, still mobile, with a form and basic SEO. The plus means the figure moves with the features you add.

Domain registration, hosting and paid stock photographs are not included unless that is written down. Payments for these packages go through PayPal. The enquiry on the finished site can point at whatever channel you actually use. This studio’s own site lists an email assembled in the browser and the enquiry form, and it does not publish a phone number.

What the build includes

  • A mobile-friendly build of the agreed pages.
  • An enquiry path: a form, a clear contact link, or both.
  • Basic SEO: titles, descriptions and readable URLs. Not a promise of rankings.
  • One review cycle after the first build, then go-live once the balance is paid.
  • Demo themes you can look at before choosing a direction. They are samples, not client projects.

There is no DWG deliverable for a website. If you also need drawings, add marble and stone shop drawings or architectural drafting to the same enquiry and they will be quoted on their own.

How a site job runs, including across the emirates

The steps are the same whether the business is in Dubai, Abu Dhabi or Sharjah, because the site is delivered online. You pay the 50% deposit, send logos, text and photographs, the pages are built, you review them, and the balance is paid when the site is ready to go live. Nothing here is a claim about a trade-licence display rule or a tourism permit. If your activity needs a particular disclaimer or a licence number on the page, send the wording and it will be placed where you ask. Content in Arabic, English or both is a normal request; say so at the start so the layout is designed for it, including right-to-left pages when Arabic is the primary language.

The package details and the live demo themes are on website packages. Drawing samples, if you want the site to point at real sheets later, are under projects.

Turnaround and the written quote

Every drawing service on this site is quoted per project. The delivery date is part of that quote and is confirmed before work starts. A comment round is a later issue, scheduled when the comments arrive. There is no published day-rate and no published square-metre rate. The AED figures elsewhere on the site — 1,200, 2,000 and 3,500+, and the 5-page contractor website at AED 2,000 with 50% upfront — are website packages only.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does the AED 2,000 package include?

The Business package is up to five pages, mobile-friendly, with a contact form and basic SEO. Half is paid upfront and the balance on completion. Domain, hosting and paid stock images are extra unless agreed in writing.

What are the other website prices?

Starter is AED 1,200 (CAD 599) for one page. Premium starts at AED 3,500 (CAD 1,799) and rises with custom features. Drawing services are not included in these figures.

Can the site be in Arabic?

Yes. Say so at the start. Arabic-first pages are laid out right to left.

Do you publish a phone number on the site you build?

Only if you want one. D3DDD’s own site does not list a phone number. Your site can use the enquiry channel you actually answer.

How does the job start?

A 50% deposit, then your logo, text and photographs. You review the build, then the balance is paid at go-live.
