About D3DDD

A Dubai studio for marble shop drawings and architectural drafting

D3DDD – Dubai 3D Design & Draft is a Dubai studio that prepares marble shop drawings, stone shop drawings, architectural drafting, quantity estimation and 3D visualization for work in Dubai and across the United Arab Emirates.

The studio was founded by Shoaib Hussan. It combines technical drafting with visual design and hands-on marble and fit-out knowledge. The aim is straightforward: documentation that is accurate, clearly organized and practical to use on site, in the workshop and in consultant review.

Who the work is for

D3DDD works with contractors, fabricators, consultants, interior designers and project teams. Typical requests include a marble or stone package that needs fabrication and installation drawings, a fit-out set that has to be coordinated before joinery is made, a quantity take-off before material is ordered, or 3D views that help a client read a design before stone is cut.

Enquiries and drawing notes can be handled in English, Arabic, or both.

Marble shop drawings and stone shop drawings

A large part of the studio’s published work is marble and stone shop drawings for villas and residences in Dubai. That work covers fabrication drawings, slab layouts, cutting lists and stone quantities. Sets are prepared so a yard can review piece sizes, joints, edges, vein direction and cutting before any slab is cut.

Selected examples are collected on the projects page. They include waterjet medallion and flooring layouts, bathroom wall and floor cladding, vanity tops, slab-by-slab cutting plans, door-frame and skirting nesting, and external pool and terrace tiling details. Client names and identifying details have been removed from every sheet.

If you need stone shop drawings for a yard or marble contractor, the same service is described for that audience on shop drawings for UAE marble contractors.

Architectural drafting, designing and fit-out

Architectural drafting covers shop drawings, CAD detailing, as-built work, joinery and fit-out sets for Gulf construction and interior work. Fit-out and joinery shop drawings are used for ceilings, partitions, kitchens, wardrobes and interior elevations.

Where a project still needs design development rather than fabrication drawings, architectural designing covers concept layouts, space planning, elevations and design development. One published example is a colour-zoned villa ground-floor flooring layout prepared as a design option for client approval, before detailed marble shop drawings.

Quantity estimation, 3D visualization and contractor websites

Architectural estimation covers bills of quantities (BOQ), quantity take-offs, material schedules and tender packages measured from project drawings. This sits beside marble shop drawings when a stone order has to be quantified before cutting.

3D rendering, modeling and visualization is used for still renders, material studies and presentation views — useful when a client or consultant needs to see stone, joinery or a space before fabrication. The studio also builds contractor and business websites for Gulf firms; published package prices for websites are listed on that page. Shop-drawing and render fees for drafting work are quoted per project.

How drawings are issued

Drawing sets are issued as AutoCAD DWG and PDF. Notes can be written in English, Arabic, or both. The studio’s published approach on this page is precision (accurate dimensions and annotations), clarity (organized sheets that are easier to coordinate) and responsiveness (timely replies by email in English and Arabic).

Work is delivered digitally. D3DDD is based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. There is no published street address and no public phone number. Areas served, as listed on the site, are Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Umm Al Quwain and Fujairah.

To ask for a quote, send drawings, sketches, dimensions or a short project description through the contact form. Say whether you need marble shop drawings, architectural drafting, quantity estimation, 3D visualization, or a mix.
