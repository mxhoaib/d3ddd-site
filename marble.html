What a marble shop drawing is for

Marble and stone shop drawings are the fabrication and installation set for a specific job. They sit between the interior designer’s intent and the bridge saw. A countertop, a vanity, a stair, a bookmatched wall or a floor layout each needs its own information: plan, elevations, sections, edge, thickness, finish, joint, and every cut-out. The What are marble shop drawings? explains the idea in more detail, and the UAE marble shop-drawing checklist is the short list fabricators actually tick.

D3DDD draws these sets in AutoCAD for contractors and stone workshops working in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and across the UAE. The price is quoted per project after the drawings are reviewed. Nothing on this page is a rate card. Send the architectural set, the interior elevations, slab photos or a measured sketch through the enquiry form.

Deliverables

  • Shop drawings: plans, elevations, sections and enlarged details with stone name, thickness, edge and finish.
  • Fabrication drawings: mitres, tolerances, joints, and the CNC or waterjet notes the workshop asked for.
  • Cutting lists and slab layouts that follow the vein, including bookmatching where the design depends on it.
  • Quantity take-off and an Excel-ready summary when estimation is part of the quote.
  • DWG plus PDF, with a revision table. Arabic notes on request, alongside or instead of English.

File formats

DWG is the file the factory keeps. PDF is the file the consultant marks and the site prints. If the quote includes a cutting list or a bill of quantities, that schedule is laid out for a spreadsheet. Renderings of a feature, when they are part of the same job, are images, not a substitute for the measured sheets. If a workshop needs a particular layer name or a title block, send a sample and the set will follow it.

From enquiry to installation

  1. Send the design drawings, sizes and any slab photos.
  2. The scope, price and delivery date are confirmed in writing.
  3. The first issue goes out as DWG and PDF.
  4. You, the fabricator or the consultant comment.
  5. The revision is issued, then the workshop cuts and the site installs.

Turnaround is the date in that written quote. A pair of vanities is a smaller set than a villa ground floor with waterjet and bookmatched walls. A consultant’s comment round is another issue, not a surprise rewrite of the fee, and it is timed when the comments arrive. If a site size is still open, that sheet is flagged and left until the size is known.

Consultant review, in general terms

In Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah the stone shop drawings are usually one package inside the contractor’s submission to the project consultant. The reviewer checks them against the interior elevations: joint positions, edge profiles, splash heights, niche sizes, and how the stone meets joinery, glass and sanitaryware. Comments return on the PDF. The revised issue carries a new revision number so the factory does not cut from a superseded sheet. Some projects want a transmittal and a fixed title block; some only want the PDF by email. Send whatever rule your project already uses. D3DDD does not invent an authority submission, and these drawings are not a permit set. If the same project also needs coordinated architectural sheets, that work sits under architectural drafting.

Staircases need tread, riser and fixing information that a floor plan will not carry. The note on stone staircase shop drawings walks through that kind of sheet. Slab nesting — which piece comes from which slab so the vein continues — is covered in slab nesting and cutting lists.

Work you can look at

The projects are real sheets with client marks removed: bathroom marble cladding, Venatino bath vanity, Paonazzetto vanity tops, bathroom slab nesting, Fusion Blue door frames and skirting and entrance lobby waterjet medallion. Floor layouts such as villa ground-floor flooring layout and external work such as pool and outdoor majlis tiling show the same drafting habit on a larger plan. If the job is still at concept stage, architectural designing and 3D rendering can sit alongside the shop drawings so the client sees the material before it is approved for cutting.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What do you need before quoting marble shop drawings?

The design drawings or sketches, stone name and thickness if you already know them, slab photos when vein matching matters, and the areas in scope. A deadline helps. Send them through the enquiry form.

Which formats are delivered?

AutoCAD DWG and PDF as standard. Cutting lists and quantity summaries are spreadsheet-ready when they are included in the quote. Other formats are only agreed if you ask for them.

Do you draw vanities, floors, stairs and cladding?

Yes. Countertops, vanities, staircases, wall cladding, flooring, kitchen islands, fireplaces and waterjet inlays are the usual scope. Each is quoted from the drawings you send.

Who approves the stone drawings in Dubai, Abu Dhabi or Sharjah?

Typically the project consultant named by the contractor: architect, interior designer or engineer. The exact checklist, title block and number of copies come from that project. Send them if you have them. Approval to fabricate is the reviewer’s, not a municipal stamp on the shop drawing.

Can the notes be in Arabic?

Yes. Notes can be English, Arabic or bilingual. Say which when you enquire.

Is estimation included?

Quantity take-off and an Excel-ready cost summary are available and priced with the drawing scope. They are not added silently. See architectural estimation if the package is a full BOQ rather than a stone schedule.
